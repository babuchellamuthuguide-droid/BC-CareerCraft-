        Privacy and your data
        • Everything you type, including your photo, stays in your own browser. This website does not upload it to any server.
        • Your draft is autosaved in this browser so you do not lose your work. "Clear form" deletes it.
        • On a shared or public computer, click "Clear form" when you finish.
        • The college list comes from public government data (AISHE, Ministry of Education).
        • If you send feedback through the feedback form, only what you write there is shared with us. Please do not include your phone number or ID details.
